In Ice We Steel

Rise of Kerti #1

Released: November 13, 2025

Cover design by Adam Fyda.

100% human art. No gen AI.


Rebellions aren’t born. They are forged.

On Kerti Island, where knowledge is scarce and belongs solely to the regime, Zea is a Truth Seeker: scholar, polymath, advisor, prisoner—born and raised to serve. But she is done serving. Her fate will be hers to decide. And her plan might just be the spark that ignites a revolution.

Ax, a thief, doesn’t care about rebellion—only money. But what if even the right price can’t buy freedom under tyranny?

Veyre, a soldier, is sworn to uphold order, hunting down rebels without question. But can she keep her honor when the orders no longer demand loyalty—but her conscience?

Tricks, lies, and secrets uphold the Great Leaders’ regime. And tricks, lies, and secrets will bring it to its knees.

But for Kerti Island to rise, they need more than hope. More than a plan. More than a spark. Above all—they need the right story.

And sometimes, a story is all it takes.


For readers who loved a high-stakes rebellion in Andor, themes of oppression, lies, and truth found in 1984, Handmaid's Tale, and Blood Over Bright Haven, and heists and found family of Six of Crows.

About the Author


IN ICE WE STEEL is a work of passion, created by an author who herself experienced the rise of a tyrannical regime in her country and had to immigrate to escape it. Ayleen grew up listening to her grandparents' stories about their life under USSR oppression and has seen firsthand the polarization and distortion of truth through propaganda.


Ayleen is a passionate reader and writer. She loves nature, especially trees, horses, and dogs.


In her time apart from working, writing, and reading, Ayleen loves to learn more about the world, watch series, travel, ride horses, draw, and play padel and board games with friends.
